## About

I am a poet, artist, researcher, and theorist working across emerging and frontier technologies, arts and cultural practice, strategy and policy, pedagogy, and participatory research.

My work asks how technologies become cultural systems, how people are invited into technical futures, and how creative practice can function as a form of research, literacy, and worldmaking.
